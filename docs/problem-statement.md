# Problem statement version-1:

## Initial user
Students who take part in projects, courses, events, volunteering, and other learning experiences.

## Problem observed
When I tried to write my resume, I struggled to recall experiences I had completed, even though I had participated in activities and built projects.

## Desired outcome
Keep useful notes, links, and supporting files about experiences so I can find them later and describe them accurately. I also want to choose selected experiences for a portfolio and write a separate description for each.

## Current evidence
- Personal experience: I had trouble recalling my work while writing my resume.
- Other students: I plan to speak with friends to learn whether they have the same problem.
- Open question: How often do students lose track of notes, links, files, or details about their contributions?

## Assumptions to investigate
- Students forget useful details when they wait until resume-writing time to record them.
- Keeping notes and evidence together would make past work easier to recall.
- Students want control over which experiences and files are public.
